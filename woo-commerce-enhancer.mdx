## Display "Free" Instead of "\$0" for Free Products

By default, WooCommerce products that have no cost will display "\$0" instead of "Free." While this approach is fine for some stores, it's not ideal for others.

## Smart Coupon Links

Add a discount code to a product URL, and it will automatically apply the discount for the customer. They land on your site, browse, and add items, and the discount stays with them.

You can build **Smart Coupon Links by hand** (e.g., https://yourstore.com/checkout/?add-to-cart=123456&coupon=SPRING50) or use the Link Management feature of our [WordPress Enhancer](https://docs.pinedigital.co/wordpress-enhancer) plugin (Link Management → Link Generator → Coupon**)** to easily create them.

## Autocomplete Order Status

Automatically assign paid WooCommerce orders a **Completed** status without requiring manual updates in the admin.

On many websites, customers buy subscriptions that do not require shipping or manual fulfillment. WooCommerce still places those orders in **Processing** after payment is received. 

**Autocomplete Order Status** addresses this issue by allowing paid orders to skip the extra admin step and go directly to **Completed** as soon as payment is confirmed. 

## Assign User Role on Product Purchase

Some websites need to assign a new role as soon as someone buys a product, but WordPress does not do that by default. **Our plugin solves this.** 

Select a product from the dropdown, choose which role to remove (optional) and which role to add, then save. When an order is marked as **Completed** for that product, the buyer’s role is automatically updated for you. Add more rules for other products as desired.
