**1.0.0 - 2026-10-7 (Initial Release)**

- **Display “Free” Instead of “\$0” for Free Products—**Shows “Free” instead of \$0 on shop and product pages
- **Smart Coupon Links—**Applies a coupon when someone visits a URL
- **Autocomplete Order Status—**Marks paid orders Completed automatically, including stuck Processing orders
- **Assign User Role on Product Purchase—**Updates the customer’s WordPress role when a completed order includes configured products
- **Filter Orders By Coupon—**Adds a coupon dropdown to the orders list to filter by the discount code used
